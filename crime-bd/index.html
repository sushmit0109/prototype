Where it is reported

Bangladesh Police reports by command, not by district. The eight metropolitan forces each cover one city; the eight Ranges cover the rest of their division. Railway Range runs along the rail network and has no territory, so it sits beside the map rather than on it.

What each place is a hotspot for

Raw counts only ever say "Dhaka is biggest". This asks a different question: given how much crime a unit records in total, which offences are over-represented in its mix compared with the national mix?

Offence concentration by unit

Each cell is a location quotient: the share of that unit's cases made up by the offence, divided by the same share nationally. 2.0 means the offence is twice as prominent in that unit's caseload as it is nationally; 0.5 means half. Population does not enter this measure, so it is unaffected by how big a unit is.

What moves together

Whether two offences rise and fall in step. Each unit's monthly series is standardised on its own history first, so the answer is not just "big places have more of everything" — it is whether an unusually heavy month for one offence tends to be an unusually heavy month for another, in the same place at the same time.

Correlation between offences

Pearson correlation across all unit-months in the selected period, after standardising each offence within each unit. Correlation is not causation: two offences can move together because one drives the other, because both follow a third thing, or because both reflect how much was being recorded that month.

How this was built, and what it cannot tell you

The source, and how it was read

Police Headquarters publishes one scanned sheet per month. There is no text layer — the numbers exist only as pixels — so every figure here was read off an image.

What makes that trustworthy is that each sheet over-determines itself. Every row prints a Recovery sub-total and a grand total; the bottom row totals every column. That is 53 independent arithmetic constraints per sheet. A transcription is only accepted when all 53 balance to zero residual, so a misread digit cannot pass silently.

Corrections carried on the record

The 2020–May 2025 figures came from an earlier compilation that had itself been OCR'd, and it carried the marks of it: a capital I standing in for the digit 1, scanner speckle read as a decimal point or a minus sign, two rows dropped altogether. Each was re-read from the original PDF and is listed here rather than quietly patched.

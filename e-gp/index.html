Bangladesh e-GP Procurement Tracker

Every award on the national e-Government Procurement portal, cross-referenced against the debarment register: which debarred companies won government contracts anyway, and when. Built from eprocure.gov.bd, the source CPTU publishes.

Debarments by severity

The source gives no severity rating of its own. This combines how broad the debarment's scope is (a Single Tender ban vs. a nationwide e-GP Portal ban), how long it runs, and whether the same company has been debarred more than once -- a first-pass heuristic, not an official rating.

Flagged awards

Contracts awarded to a company matched against the debarment register by normalised name, corroborated by district (the contract's district must match a district taken from the debarment record's own address) -- and only where that name isn't spread across more than 5 districts nationwide under other, unrelated contracts, which is the signature of a common name pattern rather than one company. Both checks exist because an earlier, name-only pass produced thousands of "violations" dominated by common surname+"Enterprise" style names shared by many unrelated small firms. Active violation means the contract was signed while the debarment was in force. Post-debarment award means it was signed after the debarment period ended (recidivism tracking, not a violation by itself). Treat every row as a lead to verify against the source, not a proven finding.
